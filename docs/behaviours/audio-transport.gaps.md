The model emits physical publications (line, reply and exact instant), staged/observed
requests and timer observations. U1 must encode original cue bytes at the port boundary and
retain replies; a later mixer consumes musical/effect results separately. No production
consumer currently holds these outputs. No existing audio implementation is replaced.

Fresh construction validates the two-track state/content relation, saved phrase positions,
complete ordered effect histories, pitch domains and resolved instrument-work classes. A
restored State resumes with the same validated resident content identity; it is not reinitialized
or supplied with replacement content. The harness retains one immutable Content for every
saved continuation. Instants and elapsed audio intervals use the shared rational
`AudioInstant` type, measured in audio cycles; `AudioCycles` names whole musical/effect work.
Sender work uses CPU master ticks and requires a separately admitted clock conversion.

Effect timbres classify admitted instrument work. Retained effect-instrument playback output
is outside this transport boundary; a future audio backend needs its own modeled owner. Music
instrument retention is compared separately.

until their independent content, clocks and loaded transport states are decoded. Decode or
admission failures are defects, not successful skips. Constructed vectors establish no native
row coverage. Whole CPU timing coverage remains 0/140; the ordinary diagnostic opening still
