# Running and jumping (a clean description)

Samus stands still until the player holds left or right. Holding a direction makes her run:
her speed climbs each frame until it reaches a top speed, and letting go makes her slow down
and stop. Holding the run button lets her run faster, and with the speed booster she keeps
gaining speed until she glows and smashes through blocks. Turning around on the ground takes a
few frames, and she slides a little the old way while she turns.

Pressing jump lifts her off the ground. The longer the button is held, the higher she rises;
releasing it early cuts the jump short. A spin jump while moving sideways flips her through the
air, and with the space jump she can jump again at the top of each spin. Next to a wall, a spin
jump away from it is a wall jump. With the hi-jump boots she rises higher. Underwater, without
the gravity suit, she moves slowly and falls gently.

Rolling into the morph ball lets her fit through gaps one tile high. The ball bounces twice
when it lands from a fall, and the spring ball lets her jump while rolled up. Landing on a
slope, she follows its surface. When she walks off a ledge she starts to fall, faster each
frame up to a limit, and she lands when her feet meet the floor. Hitting the ceiling stops her
rising at once.
