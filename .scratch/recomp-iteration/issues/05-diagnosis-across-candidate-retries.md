# 05: Carry diagnosis through successive candidate retries

**What to build:** A failed increment drives an evidence-based next experiment and routine repair while keeping all candidates and the accepted predecessor recoverable. Diagnostic history follows retries across immutable runs, and three consecutive experiments without new discriminating evidence produce a concrete decision brief.

**Blocked by:** 03: Prepare and accept one supported routine/width increment.

**Status:** implemented; real transient-effect execution retained by readiness feedback and final integration demonstration

- [x] Unexecuted changed keys route to covered intervals or actual removal/deferral. Panics, setup/build errors and budgets retain their stage/state/log evidence and remain error or incomplete outcomes.
