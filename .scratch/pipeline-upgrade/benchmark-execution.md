## Complete timer and comparability

The native runner starts its shared monotonic outer boundary immediately before
the public live command, after seed preparation and host preflight. It continues
through publication, final subject/verdict authentication, scoped cleanup and
deduplication, then successful public authentication of the retained verdict.
Nested and concurrent stages are reported independently; their sum is not total
wall time. The runner does not consume an orchestration lease around the live
command. Resource sampling includes its process tree and subsequent cleanup/auth
commands, with the frozen ceilings in `benchmark-budgets.md`.

Budget, correctness, input/seed drift, unexpected native-cache misses, unavailable
required resources, and host interference reject a sample as comparable evidence.
Persist failure stages and diagnostics, stop writers, complete scoped cleanup,
and authenticate any retained accepted evidence before proceeding. Failed samples
never enter a passing median. Do not silently substitute a cold sample or reduce
the workload. Record host CPU/load/memory/I/O observations throughout each sample;
PID namespace visibility alone does not establish a quiet host.

The runner's public controls exercise changed frozen input identity, changed seed
inventory, wrong capacity root, a genuinely missing cache entry, a corrupted
disposable store clone, and an actual live refusal with diagnostics/cleanup.
Use the user-approved CLI/process/metrics/evidence boundaries for TDD.

host has no `perf` executable and `perf_event_paranoid=2`; reassess at execution,

explicit limitations. Store import, restored-inventory verification, legacy
