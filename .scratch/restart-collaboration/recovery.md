## Checked recovery

1. Check the exact thread and its child/process liveness. Silence alone does
   not establish a stopped owner. Preserve active or ambiguous ownership.
2. For a confirmed stopped owner, checkpoint scoped source/WIP, evidence paths,
   claim identity and exact next step before cleanup. Retain surviving children
   and submitted work with their existing ownership.
3. Inspect process identity before stopping leftover scoped writers. Follow
   [session recovery](../../docs/agents/decomp-sessions.md#recover-a-crash) and
   [run cleanup](../../docs/agents/run-cleanup.md) for checked transfer, scoped
   reclamation and worktree retirement. Preserve valid evidence, prepared
   libraries and other owners' source/indexes.
4. Reassign the exact unfinished scope through supported agent tooling and the
   current owner. Record the replacement's real identity, writable paths,
   retained checkpoint and next command. A previously failed session is history
   after transfer; direct followups go through the replacement owner.

Recovery is complete when each unfinished scope has one observed owner, the
replacement can resume from the retained checkpoint, and stopped writers and
disposable artifacts have recorded cleanup. Resume authorized work without
another permission request or unnecessary verification rerun. Keep restart
support separate from original-production claims and authority.
